SOORYA BANDA

Directed by Ranjith Shetty Kukkude

"Soorya Banda" (The Sun Arrives) is a profound exploration of hope and dawn in the human psyche. Through a blend of traditional folklore and experimental stagecraft, it tells the story of a village waiting for a light that never fades.

SOORYA CHANDRA

Directed by Rohith S Baikady

A dialogue between the celestial and the mortal, "Soorya Chandra" is a visual spectacle of duality. Weaving together shadow play and intense dramatic movement, it portrays the eternal dance of light and shade.
